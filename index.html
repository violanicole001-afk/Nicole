Architectural Dossier Pre-Colonial - Present

The Bahay Kubo (Nipa Hut) is an iconic stilted indigenous house crafted entirely from local organic materials. Engineered for humid tropical monsoons, its light flexible bamboo frame withstands earthquakes while elevated lantay floors permit cooling airflow.

Ecological Wisdom

Traditional dwellings utilize zero-carbon local bio-materials (nipa, bamboo, cogon, volcanic stone) suited to microclimates.

Social Cohesion (Bayanihan)

Structures like the Bahay Kubo were lightweight enough to be moved bodily by neighbors, establishing communal unity.

Climate Resilience

From Ivatan typhoon-proof meter-thick limestone walls to Ifugao rat-proof halipan discs, design reflects survival innovation.
